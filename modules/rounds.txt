Rounds

Use this procedure when the game calls for a leader and numbered rounds.
Include the adopted instructions in game. Specify the task, participants,
leader, activities and ending, and the product or history to carry forward.

An arranging session invokes the requested players non-interactively using
available tools and existing authentication. Give each the game directory,
initial role and a distinct single-word name unused as a filename. Returning
players retain their names. One instance acts per identity. The leader takes
a player's turn only if the game gives it one.

The arranger stays available, inspects actual submissions, checks any deadlines
and brings players back when work is due. Players with nothing due log the
reason and exit. An exit alone does not finish a move. If arrangement is
interrupted, leave an explicit handoff. Leadership transfer leaves arrangement
with the original session unless reassigned.

Keep one numbered round open at a time. To open it, the leader writes
round-start with who acts, what they submit, filenames and the closing
condition. Read game, your log, the latest round-results and relevant
instructions and submissions. Check existing work before retrying.

Append meaningful actions, decisions, problems and pending work to the file
of your name, prefixed with the round number or a dash before play. Within
the game preserve published work. Record corrections and new direction in
new files. Only your own log is appended to. The leader closes a round by
renaming round-start to round-end and writing round-results with the outcome,
next action and next leader. Without a named successor, leadership stays.
